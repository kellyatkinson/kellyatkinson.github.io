Profile

Olympic recurve archer based in Auckland, New Zealand, competing in World Archery target and field events and in IFAA field disciplines.

Ten years ago I picked up a bow and have grown through regional, national, and now international championships. Most recently I successfully defended the World Champion title at the 2026 IFAA World Field Archery Championships. I shoot New Zealand record scores at IFAA and World Archery field events.

I split my time between outdoor 70 m target seasons, indoor seasons, and field training in and near Auckland with a focus on consistency, technical refinement, and competition readiness. My coach is James Gaze.
